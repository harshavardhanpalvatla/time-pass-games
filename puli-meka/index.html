ఆడు పులి ఆట — Tigers & Goats

How to Play

Tigers (3) start at three corners. Goats (15) enter one per turn.
Phase 1: Goats place on empty nodes. Tigers move or capture.
Phase 2: Both sides move. Tigers jump over adjacent goats to capture them.
Tigers win: capture 5 goats.   Goats win: trap all tigers with no moves.
